RSI — Relative Strength Index
In plain words

Compares recent gains with recent losses, from 0 to 100. Above 70 is often read as stretched. NVDA is at 64.

How it's calculated
RSI = 100 − 100 ÷ (1 + avg gain ÷ avg loss)
